I am a **GenAI / LLM Application Engineer & Data Science Engineer** based in Orange County, CA. With a Master of Science in Computer Science from the **University of Southern California (USC)** and over 10 years of software engineering background spanning mobile development and rigorous Quality Assurance engineering, I bring a unique combination of algorithmic depth and software reliability to AI systems.
